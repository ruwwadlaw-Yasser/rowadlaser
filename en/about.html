We build trust before we shape metal

Ruwwad Laser is a Saudi company born from a simple idea: that every client should find someone to execute their metal request with quality, speed, and a fair price.

Our story

From idea to leadership

We noticed that many clients — individuals and contractors alike — struggle to find a reliable workshop that executes their metal requests with the required speed and quality, especially for small or unusual orders.

So Ruwwad Laser came to be the trusted link: we take your request, choose the best execution through our network of specialized workshops, and follow quality through to delivery — so you get a perfect result without the hassle of searching and comparing.

Our mission

To deliver precise, fast metal solutions that meet the needs of industrial, commercial, and construction sectors — with full commitment to quality, deadlines, and long-term client relationships.

Our vision

To be the first and most trusted choice for laser metal cutting, forming, and fabrication solutions in the Kingdom — the reference for anyone seeking precision, speed, and quality.
