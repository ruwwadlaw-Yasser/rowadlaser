Why stainless is cut with a laser specifically

Stainless steel resists rust thanks to a thin chromium-oxide layer that forms on its surface. Any cutting method that causes excess heat or iron contamination can weaken that layer at the edge, so rust appears later exactly at the cut — the most common complaint in cheap stainless work.

A laser works with a very narrow heat-affected zone, and with the right inert assist gas the edge comes out clean — no black oxidation, no contamination from steel tooling. The result is a part that keeps its corrosion resistance in service, and an edge that needs no grinding that would dull its shine.

That is why we use the laser for every stainless job with a visible finish: façades, restaurant fittings, worktops, plaques and balustrades — where the edge itself is part of the product, not something hidden.

304 vs 316 in one sentence

  • 304 is the standard choice: corrosion resistant in normal indoor and outdoor use, better priced, and it covers most kitchen and décor work.
  • 316 contains molybdenum, which gives it higher resistance to chlorides — salty air, high humidity and aggressive cleaning agents.
  • 430 is magnetic, lower in nickel and cheaper, used for interior surfaces not exposed to constant moisture.
  • In Jeddah specifically — a coastal city — we recommend 316 for anything installed outdoors or near the sea, and 304 for interiors.

Grades and finishes

Stainless grades and where they are used

Choosing grade and finish before cutting saves you a replacement cost later. This is what we base our recommendation on.

Grade / finishPropertiesRecommended use
304 (18/8)Good corrosion resistance, easy to bend and weld, generally non-magnetic.Kitchens, furniture, interior décor, worktops, general equipment.
316 / 316LHigher resistance to chlorides and chemicals thanks to molybdenum.Coastal environments, bathrooms, pools, chemical and medical applications.
430Ferritic and magnetic, less nickel, cheaper, less corrosion resistant than 304.Interior panels, covers and dry applications not in constant water contact.
2B finishA smooth, semi-bright surface — the most available and lowest cost.Parts that will be coated or hidden, or where final appearance does not matter.
BrushedDirectional grain lines that hide fingerprints and light scratches.Façades, restaurant fittings, plaques — anything the customer sees.
MirrorA highly reflective surface that shows every scratch and fingerprint.Premium décor and feature plaques, kept in protective film until installation.

Available thicknesses and grades vary by order and material availability — send your spec and we confirm availability and price before starting.
