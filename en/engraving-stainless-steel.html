Laser engraving on stainless steel

Stainless is the most requested metal for engraving — it gives the highest contrast and the cleanest result. A deep black mark stands out against the silver surface and stays legible after years of washing and cleaning, without opening a door to rust.

Why stainless gives the best engraving result

Stainless steel has a light, uniform surface. When a fibre laser beam hits it with tuned settings, a very thin oxide layer forms that appears deep black. The result is a sharp contrast between the black mark and the silver metal — the clearest achievable on any metal.

More importantly: this black mark removes no material and does not breach the chromium-oxide layer that gives stainless its corrosion resistance. So a stainless part working in a kitchen, a humid environment, or even in food contact can be marked without the marked area becoming a weak point where corrosion starts.

When we choose black marking and when we choose recessed engraving

  • Black marking: for visible parts, products, medical tools and restaurant fittings — visually better and completely safe for corrosion resistance.
  • Recessed engraving: for parts exposed to abrasion or constant abrasive cleaning, where the text must stay readable even if the surface wears.
  • Deep engraving: for moulds and heavy spare parts, or when the surface will be coated later and the mark must show through.
  • In Jeddah specifically — a coastal city — we recommend surface marking for any stainless part installed outdoors or near the sea, to avoid touching the protective layer.
