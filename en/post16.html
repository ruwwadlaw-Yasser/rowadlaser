Ruwwad Laser: who we are, and how we turn your idea into a finished metal part

Ruwwad Laser is a Saudi company based in Jeddah, specialized in laser cutting, forming, and fabrication of metal and stainless steel, along with high-precision metal engraving and marking. In this article we introduce ourselves in detail: who we are, what we offer, how we work, and why contractors, factory owners, shop owners, and project teams with repeat orders choose us — and at the end you will find our full company profile as a downloadable PDF.

Who we are

We combine experience with modern technology to deliver metal products that meet the highest quality standards, with full commitment to precision and speed. It starts with your idea, your engineering drawing, or even a rough concept — and we turn it into a well-made metal part, produced with care and reviewed at every stage. We care about the smallest details, and we believe quality is measured not only by the final result, but by how you get there.

Because our clients' needs differ from one project to the next, we offer flexible solutions for industrial and commercial projects and for custom requests — from a trial batch to large-volume and repeat production. Every project gets the same attention, the same commitment, and the same care to arrive on the agreed date with the quality our clients' trust deserves.

At Ruwwad Laser we do not just execute metalwork — we aim to build long-term partnerships based on trust, professionalism, and commitment, so we become the first choice for anyone looking for precision and quality in metal fabrication.

Our mission, vision, and values

  • Our mission: precise, fast metal solutions for the industrial, commercial, and construction sectors, with full commitment to quality and deadlines, and long-term client relationships.
  • Our vision: to be the first and most trusted choice for laser metal cutting, forming, and fabrication in the Kingdom — the reference for anyone seeking precision, speed, and quality.
  • Our values: Precision — built exactly to the drawing. Speed — clear dates and real commitment to delivery. Trust — transparent pricing and personal follow-up. Quality — industrial standards and inspection before every delivery.

Our services: complete metal solutions under one roof

1. CNC laser cutting

Fast, accurate sheet-metal cutting with clean edges and complex detail, built to the drawing to within ±0.1 mm. CNC control means part number one and part number one hundred are identical — which matters when parts must fit together on site.

2. Metal forming and bending

Bending and folding sheets at precise angles to produce 3D parts and frames ready for installation — from simple brackets and supports to enclosures and assembled structures.

3. Stainless steel fabrication

Products, equipment, furniture, and restaurant and kitchen fittings with a professional finish that resists corrosion and heavy use: work tables, shelving, hoods, sinks, and frames for restaurants, cafés, healthcare, and industry.

4. Fiber laser engraving and marking

Engraving names, logos, serial numbers, and QR codes on metal — for plates, signage, gifts, industrial parts, and production numbering. The result is a permanent mark that does not fade with use.

5. Industrial parts and spares

Precision parts made to engineering drawings and required dimensions for factories, workshops, and production lines — including remaking a discontinued part from a sample or a drawing.

6. Façades and decorative metalwork

Storefront façades, signage, decorative panels, and architectural metal elements with a lasting professional look — whether laser-cut patterns or solid panels in different finishes.

The metals we work with

We work with more than ten metal types in multiple thicknesses, and we select the metal and thickness to suit the part's end use — not just what happens to be available:

  • Stainless steel (304 / 316) — for fittings, kitchens, and humid or saline environments.
  • Mild and carbon steel — for frames, structural parts, and load-bearing components.
  • Aluminum — light and corrosion-resistant, suited to façades and lightweight parts.
  • Copper and brass — for décor, plates, and pieces with a distinctive character.
  • Galvanized sheet and coated metals — for outdoor and long-life applications.

The sectors we serve

  • Industrial: spare parts, frames, and production-line components for factories and workshops.
  • Commercial: façades, signage, decorative metal, and shop and restaurant fittings.
  • Construction: structural and architectural elements for contractors and property projects.
  • Projects and branches: custom and repeat requests — plates, logos, or a special design.

How to order: from idea to delivery in four steps

  • Send your request — via WhatsApp, email, or the quote form on the site, attaching whatever you have: a drawing, a sketch, or even a photo with dimensions. If you have no drawing, we will help you prepare one.
  • A fast quote — we review the details and send a clear price and a defined schedule, with no obligation on your side.
  • Production — cutting, forming, and fabrication usually start within 6 hours of approval, with quality followed step by step.
  • Inspection and delivery — we check dimensions and finish before delivery, and hand over the finished product on the agreed date.

What we need to issue a quote

  • The drawing or sketch — or a clear photo with dimensions.
  • Metal type and thickness — if you are unsure, we will advise.
  • The quantity — from a trial batch to project volumes.
  • Your deadline — so we can plan the schedule around it.

The clearer this information is, the faster and more accurate the quote — and the fewer review rounds that delay production.

Why our clients choose us

  • Speed: a quick reply, a fast quote, production starting within 6 hours of approval, and dates we actually keep.
  • Flexibility: we take on unusual, spec-driven orders — from a trial batch to volume production.
  • Breadth: many metals and services under one roof, so you are not chasing multiple suppliers for one job.
  • Precision: built to the drawing, clean edges, exact dimensions, and 100% inspection before delivery.
  • Trust and personal follow-up: transparent pricing and personal follow-up on every order from the first message to delivery.
