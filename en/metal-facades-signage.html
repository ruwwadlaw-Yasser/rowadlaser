Why metal signage fades in Jeddah

Jeddah combines three harsh conditions for any sign: direct sun most of the year, high humidity, and air carrying salt from the sea. Signage that survives in a dry inland city can start rusting or peeling here within months — and the cause is always one of three: the wrong metal, a coating not made for outdoors, or fixings in a different metal that react with the panel.

So every signage order starts with a question about the exact installation spot: is it on a façade facing the street, inside a covered walkway, close to the corniche? The answer sets the metal (316 stainless, aluminium, or powder-coated steel) and the fixing type — and that decision matters more than the design itself in determining how long the sign lasts.

As for the design, we are flexible: send your logo in any format and we convert it into a cutting file. Logos with very fine detail or thin strokes may need a small adjustment to stay producible and mountable, and we show you that adjustment for approval before production.

The signage types we produce

  • Individual cut letters mounted directly on the wall or on a backing, with depth that casts a shadow.
  • A full panel with the logo cut out of it, so the background or lighting shows through.
  • A dimensional logo with folded-return letters (box letters) giving more depth and rigidity.
  • Interior wayfinding: door numbers, department names and entrance plaques, with text fibre-engraved.

Choosing the façade metal

Which metal for your sign?

The installation spot decides the metal more than the shape or the budget does.

Sign locationRecommended metalWhy
Outdoor façade near the sea316 stainlessThe highest resistance to chlorides in salty air, holding its look for years.
Outdoor façade inland304 stainless or aluminiumAdequate protection at lower cost, with aluminium lighter for large suspended letters.
A sign in brand coloursSteel or aluminium with powder coatingThe brand colour can be matched precisely with RAL codes — bare metal cannot.
Interior sign in a mallBrushed stainless or aluminiumA sheltered environment, so the focus is appearance and weight rather than corrosion.
A warm premium accentCopper or brassA distinctive golden tone, bearing in mind it will change gradually outdoors.
