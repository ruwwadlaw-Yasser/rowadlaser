Materials

Metals we work with

We work with 10+ metal types in multiple thicknesses, and we will advise on the best metal and thickness for your request if you are unsure.

  • Stainless steel (304 / 316) — for fittings, kitchens, and humid environments.
  • Mild and carbon steel — for frames and structural parts.
  • Aluminum — light and corrosion-resistant, for façades and lightweight parts.
  • Copper and brass — for décor, plates, and premium pieces.
  • Galvanized sheet and coated metals — for outdoor applications.
