Mild steel: when is it the right choice?

Mild (carbon) steel is the most used metal in fabrication for one simple reason: its strength-to-price ratio beats every other metal. A load-bearing part, a base plate anchored into concrete, a frame that will be clad later — all are made in steel, because any alternative would double the cost with no real benefit.

Its only drawback is that it rusts — and that is entirely solvable: powder coating, primer plus topcoat, or hot-dip galvanising for parts that will live outdoors. What matters is planning the protection from the start, not reacting after rust appears.

Practically, steel is the easiest metal to laser cut: it absorbs the beam efficiently and gives a straight edge at a higher cutting speed — which makes it the most economical metal for large, repeating orders.

Hot-rolled or cold-rolled?

  • Hot-rolled: thicker sections, a mill-scale surface, and a lower price — suited to frames and base plates that will be painted.
  • Cold-rolled: a cleaner, smoother surface and tighter dimensions — suited to covers and visible parts that will take a fine finish coat.
  • If the part will be hidden inside an assembly, do not pay the premium for a cleaner surface — tell us the use and we will recommend the cheaper option.

Protection after cutting

How to protect steel from rust

A steel part needs one decision after cutting: how will it be protected? Here are the common options and where each fits.

Protection methodWhat it givesBest for
Primer + topcoatGood protection at low cost, and easy to touch up locally when scratched.Interior parts and frames sheltered from rain and direct moisture.
Powder coatingA uniform, hard, scratch-resistant layer in RAL colours with consistent gloss.Visible parts: signage, furniture, covers and interior balustrades.
Hot-dip galvanisingA zinc layer that protects even when scratched and lasts years outdoors.Outdoor and structural parts exposed to rain and constant humidity.
No coatingSurface rust starts within weeks in Jeddah’s humid climate.Temporary parts, prototypes, or anything that will be enclosed immediately.

The quote states clearly whether protection is included or separate, so there are no surprises in the final cost.
