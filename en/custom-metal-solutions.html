Why custom requests usually get refused

Many shops work with standard, off-the-shelf products. When a project needs parts with a custom size or design — even in volume — the client hears: we do not do this type, it will take too long, or simply gets no reply.

We treat these requests as a core part of our work, not an exception. Your small order today may become a repeat project a year from now — and more importantly, the real value of the service shows when you need something you cannot buy off the shelf, not when you order what is available everywhere.

And we do not expect you to be an engineer. Send what you have: a phone photo, a sketch on paper, a tape-measure dimension, or even a description in words. We ask the questions we are missing, prepare the drawing ourselves, and show it to you for approval before any metal is cut.

Custom requests we actually receive

  • A discontinued part on an old machine with no market replacement — reproduced from the original or its dimensions.
  • A cover or bracket in a non-standard size with no off-the-shelf equivalent, made to the exact dimension needed.
  • A part bought ready-made that fails after months, remade in a metal or thickness better suited to service conditions.
  • A design idea for a new product where the owner wants one sample to test before committing to volume.
Request types

How your custom request can start

It does not matter where you start — all of these routes end with a finished metal part.

You have a ready drawing

A DXF, DWG or PDF from an engineer or designer — we review it technically, price it and start.

You have a sketch or a photo

A pencil drawing or a phone photo with basic dimensions — we turn it into a producible drawing.

You have the old part

Send the part itself or its exact dimensions, and we produce a matching or improved copy.

You only have an idea

Describe the function you need and the space available, and we propose a shape and a workable solution.

You have a space needing a solution

An awkward corner in a kitchen, shop or workshop that no stock product fits — we build what does.

You have an occasion or a gift

An award, plaque or keepsake with a personal design, cut and engraved with a name and date.

How we work

From idea to part in four steps

Send what you have

A photo, a sketch, a description, or the part itself. Do not wait until you have a professional drawing — start with what you have.

We ask, then draw

We ask about critical dimensions, function and environment, then prepare a drawing for your approval.

Approve, then produce

Once you approve the drawing and price we start production — usually within 6 hours of approval.

Delivery and review

We deliver and confirm it actually works in place, because a custom part is judged by how it performs in use.

Questions we usually ask — answering them speeds things up

  • What exactly does the part do — what does it carry, cover or fix?
  • Which dimensions cannot change, and which are flexible?
  • Where will it be used: indoors, outdoors, wet, food-contact, high heat?
  • Does appearance matter, or is it a hidden functional part — and do you need one copy or more?
